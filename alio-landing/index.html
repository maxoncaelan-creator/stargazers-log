The reason clinics switch to us

We read the National Law so your marketing doesn't end up in it.

Section 133 means a regulated health service can't be advertised with testimonials — not on your homepage, not in an ad, not by resharing a glowing Google review to your feed. Most agencies discover this when the notification arrives.

We design around it from the first sketch: proof without testimonials, confidence without “best in Sydney”, outcomes without prohibited claims. Everything we ship gets a compliance pass before it goes near your domain.

What we swap it for

Credentials and scope of practice. Photographed real rooms and real staff. Plain-language explanations of what an initial consult involves. Transparent fees. Wait times. Parking, honestly.

Boring? A little. It also converts better than a five-star wall, because it answers the question the patient is actually asking.

Not legal advice — but we'll flag it before it ships.

Before you ask

Fair questions.

We're not allied health — we're a trade. Still relevant?

Very. Roughly a third of our work is plumbing, electrical, mobile mechanics and home services. Same shape of problem: skilled operator, strong word of mouth, brand that undersells the work. You skip the compliance module and we spend that time on quoting and job-booking flow instead.

Do I have to leave my current booking software?

No. We build to whatever you already run — Cliniko, Nookal, Halaxy, Power Diary, Coreplus. Changing practice software mid-rebrand is how clinics lose a month of billings, and we'll talk you out of it.

Who owns the files at the end?

You do, outright — source files, fonts licensed in your name, domain and hosting in your account. No hostage retainer. If you want us monthly afterwards, that's a separate conversation you're free to decline.

What if the audit says our brand isn't the problem?

Then we tell you that in week one and you don't spend the money. It happens a few times a year — usually the real issue is availability, cancellation policy, or a front desk that isn't rebooking. We'll write it up either way.

Six weeks — really?

Really, provided you make the four sessions. Our schedule slips when owners disappear for a fortnight mid-build, so we lock the dates at kickoff and hold them. We've run this program 64 times.

Twenty minutes, no deck, no obligation. Bring your last three months of booking numbers and we'll tell you what's leaking.
